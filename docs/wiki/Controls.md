# 🎮 Controls

## Everyday use

| Action | Default control |
| --- | --- |
| Enter a supported workstation scene | Right-click the block |
| Pick an item from the inventory panel | Click its inventory slot |
| Use the workstation | Click the highlighted area; follow the station's hint |
| Leave the scene | Esc, inventory key, or movement/jump input |
| Open the normal interface as a fallback | Sneak + right-click with an empty hand |
| Sit beside a campfire / soul campfire | Look at it and press H |
| Orbit view while working | Hold Left Alt |
| Move to a nearby station | Point at its outline and right-click when the transition hint appears |

**H** and **Left Alt** can be rebound in Minecraft's controls. Orbit view can also be changed to a toggle in the mod settings. While orbiting, move the mouse to look around and use the wheel to adjust distance; return to first person to continue interacting.

## Crafting table

Use the guide to the left of the table to search for recipes. Select a recipe to preview its ingredients; missing materials are shown on the tabletop. **Lay out** places available ingredients from your inventory, one by one. Click the floating result to craft, or use Shift for bulk crafting where supported. Ingredients remain on the table when you leave.

## Books

At a lectern, click the right or left page to turn it; the mouse wheel and arrow keys also work. Shift + click takes the book. At a chiseled bookshelf, readable books open near the shelf; right-click or Esc closes the book and puts it back, while Shift + click takes it. Closing a shelf book happens before leaving the scene.

## Special stations

Click the item name at an anvil to rename it. Scroll through stonecutter shapes and loom patterns. Select a beacon's effects, place payment, and activate it. A jukebox insertion or removal finishes with a short automatic exit. Use the on-screen hint for the exact action at the current station.
