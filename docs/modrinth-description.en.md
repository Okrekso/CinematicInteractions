# Cinematic Interactions

Put your hands to work.

Cinematic Interactions turns supported Minecraft workstations into animated first-person scenes. Step up to a crafting table, place ingredients on its surface, load a furnace by hand, or forge an item at the anvil. Your character's arms use your own skin, while camera movements bring the work into view.

[GIF 1 — Place the final ingredient on the crafting table and pick up the finished item.]

## Craft directly in the world

The crafting table becomes a visible 3×3 workspace. Its recipe guide lets you search recipes, check which materials you have, and preview ingredients directly on the tabletop. Missing materials are highlighted, and **Lay out** makes your hands place available ingredients from your inventory.

Placed ingredients stay on the crafting table when you leave and are visible to other players.

[GIF 2 — Select a recipe, show a missing ingredient, then use Lay out and craft the result.]

## Each station has its own scene

Forge with a hammer, grind a blade against the wheel, weave a banner, or watch ingredients dissolve into your potions. Read a book on a lectern with animated page turns, insert a music disc into a jukebox, or sit beside a campfire to cook.

| Activity | Supported blocks |
| --- | --- |
| Crafting and smelting | Crafting table, furnace, smoker, blast furnace, crafter |
| Tools and equipment | Anvil, grindstone, smithing table, stonecutter |
| Enchanting and brewing | Enchanting table, brewing stand |
| Banners and maps | Loom, cartography table |
| Reading and music | Lectern, jukebox |
| Campfires and beacons | Campfire, soul campfire, beacon |

## Keep your workshop flowing

Move directly between nearby supported stations with a smooth camera and hand transition. Returning to the same station shortly after leaving gives you a quicker entrance.

An inventory panel keeps your items within reach. Hold **Left Alt** to view your character at the station from third person, then release it to return to work.

[GIF 4 — Move from the crafting table to a neighboring furnace and place fuel inside.]

## Adjust the experience

Choose which blocks use scenes and adjust camera lean, zoom, entrance and exit speed, and quick return. Control hints, cinematic bars, camera motion, shake and screen flashes can be toggled individually.

Settings are available through **Mods → Cinematic Interactions → Config**. Cloth Config provides an optional settings interface; the mod also has a configuration screen without it.

## Getting started

- **Right-click** a supported block to begin.
- **Left-click / right-click** to interact with items and the station; the on-screen hints explain the available actions.
- **H** while looking at a campfire to sit beside it.
- **Right-click** a highlighted nearby station to move to it.
- **Esc, E, movement keys or jump** to leave.
- **Sneak + right-click with an empty hand** to use the usual interface where the block has one.

The campfire and third-person view keys can be rebound in Minecraft's controls.

## Installation

- **Minecraft 1.21.1**
- **NeoForge** — developed and tested with **21.1.255**
- Install the mod on **both the client and the server** for multiplayer.
- **Cloth Config is optional** and only needed on the client for its settings interface.

Chests, barrels and shulker boxes keep their usual interfaces. If a station cannot be reached or its workspace is obstructed, the default settings fall back to its usual interface where available.
